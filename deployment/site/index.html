BUILT AROUND YOUR LIBRARY

Your files.
Your choice.

Desktop: your own media.

Search runs on your computer. Local-only mode keeps processing local. In Standard mode, small keyframes and audio excerpts are sent to Nebius for NVIDIA Nemotron descriptions.

Sandbox: samples or your own uploads.

Explore shared samples or upload selected files into a private session folder. Uploads stay separate from the public library and are deleted after 24 hours. Each file can be up to 50 MB.

Uploaded files are processed on the server. Small keyframes and audio excerpts may be sent to Nebius for NVIDIA descriptions. Optional web searches send your query to Tavily. API credentials remain on the server.
