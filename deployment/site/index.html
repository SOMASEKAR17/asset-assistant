COMING NEXT / PLANNED INTEGRATIONS

From your library.
Into your workflow.

These features are on the roadmap and are not included in the current release.

01 / LOCAL MCP SERVER

Give your AI creative assistant context.

Connect your preferred MCP-compatible AI assistant to a local server that exposes your indexed library. Let it discover the footage you already have, inspect descriptions and transcripts, and find matching moments while you brainstorm.

Build scripts and scene ideas around available shots, then identify what still needs filming. Choose which library information you share with the connected assistant.

02 / VIDEO-EDITOR EXTENSIONS

Find the next shot without leaving your edit.

Search your library from a panel inside your video editor. Preview matching moments and bring selected clips into your project without switching to a separate application.

Planned integrations include Adobe Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve, with timeline context to help suggest relevant b-roll as you edit.

BUILT AROUND YOUR LIBRARY

Your files.
Your choice.

Desktop: your own media.

Search runs on your computer. Local-only mode keeps processing local. In Standard mode, small keyframes and audio excerpts are sent to Nebius for NVIDIA Nemotron descriptions.

Sandbox: samples or your own uploads.

Explore shared samples or upload selected files into a private session folder. Uploads stay separate from the public library and are deleted after 24 hours. Each file can be up to 50 MB.

Uploaded files are processed on the server. Small keyframes and audio excerpts may be sent to Nebius for NVIDIA descriptions. Optional web searches send your query to Tavily. API credentials remain on the server.
